Tired of USPS's 35‑tracking‑number limit? Manually breaking thousands of numbers into dozens of batches is exhausting.  
还在忍受 USPS 官网一次只能查 **35 单**的限制？每天手动把几千个单号分几十次粘贴，心力交瘁。

**Orange Track** was built exactly for this:  
**Orange Track** 专为解决这个痛点而生：

✅ Totally free. No sign‑up, no hidden fees, no daily limits / 真正免费，无需注册，无隐藏收费，不限次数  
✅ Paste **thousands of numbers** at once, query them all concurrently / 一次粘贴 **数千个单号**，自动并发查询  
✅ Real‑time progress with pause / resume / export to Excel / 实时进度，支持暂停/继续/导出Excel  
✅ Supports USPS, UPS, FedEx, GOFO, UniUni, DHL, OnTrac & more / 覆盖 USPS、UPS、FedEx、GOFO、UniUni、DHL、OnTrac 等

Whether you're a Temu seller, a TikTok Shop owner, or anyone shipping in bulk — Orange Track turns a 2‑hour job into 5 minutes.  
无论你是 Temu 全托管卖家、TikTok Shop 跨境店主，还是需要跟踪大批包裹的独立站运营，Orange Track 都能让你把原来 2 小时的工作压缩到 **5 分钟** 以内。

## 🧑‍💻 Quick Start / 快速开始

1. Go to **https://orange.gomioo.com** / 访问官网：**[https://orange.gomioo.com](https://orange.gomioo.com)**
2. Paste your tracking numbers (separated by newline, comma, or space) / 粘贴运单号（支持换行、逗号、空格分隔）
3. Select the carrier(s) — mixed carriers are ok / 选择承运商（支持混合查询）
4. Click "Start Tracking" and watch the progress bar / 点击「开始查询」，等待进度条跑完
5. Click "Export Excel" to download the full report / 点击「导出 Excel」下载完整结果

*Yes, it's that simple. / 是的，就是这么简单。*

## 🙋 FAQ / 常见问题

<details>
<summary>❓ Is it really free? / 真的完全免费吗？</summary>
Yes, now and for the foreseeable future. Our business model relies on other value‑added services; the core batch tracking will always be free.<br>
是的，目前以及可预见的未来都将保持免费。我们通过其他增值服务盈利，核心批量查询功能不会收费。
</details>

<details>
<summary>❓ How many numbers can I track per day? / 每天能查多少单？</summary>
No hard limit. Thousands per submission is perfectly fine. We only apply rate limiting in extreme abuse cases.<br>
没有硬性上限。一次提交几千单完全没问题。我们仅出于公平使用原则，对极端的恶意请求做限流。
</details>

<details>
<summary>❓ Is my data safe? / 数据安全吗？</summary>
We do **not store** any tracking numbers or results. All operations happen inside your browser, and the cache is cleared after you leave.<br>
我们**不存储**任何用户提交的运单号及查询结果。所有操作仅在您的浏览器内完成，查询完毕后即清空缓存。
</details>

<details>
<summary>❓ What languages are supported? / 支持哪些语言？</summary>
Currently Simplified Chinese and English. More languages are on the roadmap.<br>
目前支持简体中文和英文，后续会加入更多语言支持。
</details>
